Simple Battles
==============
Art by Hyptosis; uploaded to OpenGameArt.org by speveril.
Page: https://opengameart.org/content/simple-battles
Licence (as the page states it): CC-BY 3.0 - Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (licenses/texts/CC-BY-3.0.txt).

The page's own words (read 23 Oct 2026):
  "16 front-view "battlers", and 3 backgrounds for a simple 1 vs 1 turn-based combat system. These were all drawn by Hyptosis,
  most of them originally for SimpleQuest; a few of them were previously released. Released with permission."
  "All of the art uses Dawnbringer's 16-color palette. The scenes are vertical backgrounds, intended to fit the entire height
  and half the width of a 320x240 screen."

The zip (simple_battles.zip, SHA-256 95cb940450783eb01277ede26b29ee274b5f0c7f972545f1732bf5ece728c6f1) has only the 19 pictures,
no licence file. Manticore ships them unmodified in assets/rpg/simple_battles/; the game draws them scaled and may draw a
lighting layer over them on screen.
