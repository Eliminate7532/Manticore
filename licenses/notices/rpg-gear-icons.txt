Adventure gear icons
====================
Manticore's own pixel art (Round PROG4, 3 Nov 2026): the 13 icons of the Adventure's gear, 16 x 16 pixels each, in assets/gear/.
They are drawn by tools/rpg_gear_icons.py from character grids written for Manticore, on DawnBringer's 16-colour palette (the
palette of the Adventure's DawnLike art; a list of 16 colours, free to use). No other artwork was copied or traced.
